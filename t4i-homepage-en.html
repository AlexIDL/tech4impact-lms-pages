What is Tech4Impact

Where technical expertise meets social purpose

Tech4Impact is an Erasmus+ project connecting higher education institutions and vocational training providers across Europe to develop a new generation of socially conscious tech entrepreneurs. Whether you're a student or a professional looking to reskill, this platform is your starting point.

Choose your track

Which path fits you best?

Two distinct curricula, built around where you want to go.

HEI track

For university students in ICT, AI, and DeepTech

→ Start a social venture

Build the entrepreneurial mindset and skills to launch your own tech-driven social venture. This track prepares you to identify social problems, design impactful solutions, and lead the organisations that deliver them.

Explore the HEI track →
VET track

For professionals reskilling or upskilling

→ Work within a social venture

Develop the practical skills to contribute and grow inside social enterprises. This track is built around the realities of working within the social economy — applying digital and entrepreneurial competencies in real organisational contexts.
