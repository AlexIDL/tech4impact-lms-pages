Module 6: Foundational Soft Skills
Sections: 6
Time: 60–90 minutes

About this module

Every social venture depends on the people behind it. Technical knowledge, business planning, and financial literacy all matter — but they only go as far as the human skills that hold a team together and keep it moving.

This module focuses on the foundational soft skills that underpin effective social entrepreneurship: staying committed when things are hard, making decisions under uncertainty, listening well, working in a team, and leading one. These are skills you will use across the full curriculum — and long after the programme ends.

Meet SharedTable

Throughout this module, you will follow the story of SharedTable — a social venture building an app that connects restaurants and shops with surplus food to the people and organisations that need it. You will see the team navigate real challenges: moments of doubt, difficult decisions, friction between colleagues, and the question of who leads and how.

As you work through each section, put yourself in their shoes. The situations they face are ones you will face too.

What you will cover

  • Section 1 You and Your Mission
  • Section 2 Staying Committed
  • Section 3 Making Decisions
  • Section 4 Listening
  • Section 5 Working as a Team
  • Section 6 Leading a Team

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

  • Reflect on your personal connection to your social venture's mission
  • Apply strategies for maintaining commitment through setbacks
  • Make structured decisions under pressure and uncertainty
  • Practise active listening in team and stakeholder contexts
  • Contribute effectively as both a team member and a team leader
